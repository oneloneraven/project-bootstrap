Like any website, the service that hosts it handles your IP address and the pages you ask for, so it can deliver them and protect the site from attacks. ## Your rights

We are the controller of your data under UK data protection law, which means we're responsible for looking after it. You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, to correct it or delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to give you a copy you can take elsewhere. We reply as soon as we can, and within one month at most.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
